# Promoted online compositional temporal growth

This promotion composes the previously separate external-memory mechanisms:
fresh temporal route acquisition, repeated shared-view consolidation, and
prefix retention. Six anonymous temporal routes arrive as three distinct
shared-basis pairs. Each pair is inserted into a four-row external artifact
capacity, selected by the generic opaque consolidation policy, and reduced
from two physical rows to one while preserving two independent route views.
The compacted snapshot is explicitly grown back to the hot capacity before
the next fresh pair arrives.

Across seeds `17`, `18`, and `19`, every forward and reversed stream accepts
all three rewrites. Every earlier route remains at `1.0000` minimum fresh
verification accuracy after each later pair. Learned pair selection is
`1.0000` at all stages across the available physical permutations. The first
stage has only two candidates, so its shuffled/untrained rates are necessarily
`1.0000`; once alternatives exist, shuffled rates are
`0.8333/0.8333/0.0000` for seeds 17/18/19 at stage two and
`0.0000/0.2500/0.0000` at stage three, while untrained rates are
`0.3333` and `0.1667`. Both insertion orders preserve reload and corruption
integrity, rejected transactions are non-mutating, and the controller,
event encoder, and acquired capability file remain frozen with zero replay.

Each stream reduces serialized artifact bytes `6,338 → 3,169`,
`9,507 → 6,338`, and `12,676 → 9,507`, ending with three physical rows for
six logical routes. Per seed the run accounts for `43,904` unique temporal
verifier bits, `144` shared-view retention bits, `48` temporal logical
lifetimes, `48,000` policy-training lifetimes, `64` policy-audit lifetimes,
`48,112` total logical lifetimes, `3,000` optimizer updates, and zero replay.

This promotes bounded repeated online compositional growth with external
memory expansion and consolidation. It does not qualify unrestricted memory
growth, arbitrary new computation, semantic ontology formation, or general
continual learning. Raw reports are `seed-17.json`, `seed-18.json`, and
`seed-19.json`.
