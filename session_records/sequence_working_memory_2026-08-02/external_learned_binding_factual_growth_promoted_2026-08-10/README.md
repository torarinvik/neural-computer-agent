# Learned anonymous binding and factual-memory growth

This archive records the promoted growth rung after joint learned
binding/factual replacement. The external transaction grows both anonymous
live-track capacity and shared factual-bank capacity on isolated copies,
promotes a provisional identity only after held-out factual verification, and
commits only when all retained tracks and factual slots remain byte-stable.

Across seeds `2601`, `2602`, and `2603`:

- four anonymous streams were presented as one source and three delayed,
  interleaved open-set arrivals;
- two sequential growth transactions expanded live binding capacity from one
  to three and factual capacity from one to three;
- learned proposal selection beat both fresh and verifier-outcome-shuffled
  controls on every seed;
- scalar rejection and wrong-held-out rejection were atomic;
- the source factual slot remained unchanged and both new slots routed after
  promotion;
- the first new stream selected affine sufficient statistics and the nonlinear
  second stream selected random-feature sufficient statistics under the same
  held-out family challenger;
- exact persistence, frozen controller/encoder, zero replay, and zero factual
  optimizer updates passed.

The raw reports are `report_seed2601.json`, `report_seed2602.json`, and
`report_seed2603.json`. `sample_efficiency_ledger.json` records the required
accounting and `SHA256SUMS` protects the archive.

## Claim boundary

This promotes bounded replay-free external memory growth with retained prior
knowledge and learned anonymous proposal ranking. It does not establish
unrestricted memory growth, learned verifier design, arbitrary new computation
beyond the registered candidate families, or general continual learning.
