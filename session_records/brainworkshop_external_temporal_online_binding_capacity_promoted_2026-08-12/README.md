# Online episodic binding capacity promotion

This two-seed audit promotes the first bounded online capacity lifecycle for
the external episodic binding router. Two anonymous bindings are learned from
fresh attempted scalar utilities. Their route keys are then consolidated by
held-out copy-on-write probes. A third binding is recognized as novel, an
unsafe replacement is rejected without mutation, and the verifier-approved
candidate replaces one physical slot while retaining the sibling binding.

Across seeds 17 and 18:

- initial and consolidated binding accuracy and novelty-known rate were
  `1.0000`;
- the novel third binding had `0.0000` known rate before admission;
- accepted replacement retained the old sibling at `1.0000` and acquired the
  new binding at `1.0000`;
- the retired binding had `0.0000` known rate after replacement;
- candidate-order permutation and exact reload passed at `1.0000`;
- the learned-route reward-shuffled control was `0.5000`.

The immutable generic episode signature is deliberately separate from the
learned route embedding. It provides novelty evidence when the learned route
has not seen a future binding; the scalar-trained route remains the mechanism
used for learned selection. This is bounded external memory growth and safe
capacity reuse, not unrestricted growth, autonomous ontology formation, or
general continual learning.

## Accounting

Each seed records `6,608` unique verifier bits and logical lifetimes, `1,000`
active router updates, `512` retention-probe outcomes, `3,584` active
diagnostic outcomes, and `1,512` shuffled-control outcomes. The reload audit
reused `512` held-out examples as explicitly recorded diagnostics; no training
examples were replayed, and the controller received zero updates.
