# Joint learned binding and factual-memory lifecycle

This pressure test couples the outcome-trained anonymous binding policy to the
shared factual transition bank. A policy-selected provisional/live pair is
handled on isolated copies: the old factual slot is evicted, provisional
evidence is consumed once, an independent held-out transition is checked, and
the binding replacement plus factual promotion commit together only after the
scalar verifier outcome authorizes them.

The audit includes three simultaneous provisional identities, delayed
contradictory evidence, a wrong-held-out control, scalar-outcome rejection,
fresh-policy selection control, retained-sibling routing after replacement,
drift isolation, exact persistence, and frozen controller/encoder checks. The
drift control keeps the identity matched while the factual router emits an
actual `conflict` and leaves the factual-bank content digest unchanged. The
factual candidate uses one-pass affine sufficient statistics and zero replay.

Each seed consumes 480 policy-training verifier bits plus three transactional
verifier outcomes, for 483 unique verifier bits. The lifecycle policy and
identity encoder are trained before deployment; the factual candidate and
controller receive zero optimizer updates during the joint replacement.

This promotes only a bounded joint binding/factual transaction. It does not
establish a learned verifier, unrestricted factual-bank growth, arbitrary
drift recovery, or general continual learning.
